I study how industrial buildings behave when one hazard follows another: an earthquake, then fire.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Structural Engineering at Özyeğin University in Istanbul, advised by Asst. Prof. Derya Deniz. My thesis develops a multi-hazard performance framework for buildings exposed to fires and debris flows after earthquakes, with a focus on precast reinforced concrete industrial facilities, the backbone of Türkiye's manufacturing stock.

My work combines nonlinear numerical modelling in OpenSees and OpenSeesPy, incremental dynamic analysis, seismic and fire fragility analysis, and Bayesian updating of analytical fragility models with empirical damage data from the 2023 Kahramanmaraş earthquake sequence, where I took part in field reconnaissance of more than a hundred industrial facilities.

Before my doctorate I spent six years as a practising structural engineer designing and supervising reinforced concrete and steel buildings in Iran, which keeps my research grounded in how structures are actually designed and built.

Research

Fire following earthquake

Sequential earthquake–fire analysis of precast RC industrial buildings: how seismic damage changes thermal response, fire resistance and collapse risk.

Seismic fragility and Bayesian updating

Analytical fragility curves from incremental dynamic analysis, updated with observed damage from the 2023 Kahramanmaraş earthquakes to reduce model uncertainty.

Multi-hazard risk and resilience

Loss, impact and recovery models for industrial facilities, and frameworks for building stocks subjected to earthquakes, floods and debris flows.

Post-earthquake assessment

Field reconnaissance protocols, damage and functionality data for industrial facilities, and structural health monitoring for damage assessment.

Ground motion and uncertainty

Ground motion selection and prediction, record-to-record variability and uncertainty quantification with Monte Carlo simulation.

Performance-based design

Performance-based seismic design of industrial buildings and innovative bracing systems, including eccentric split-X braced frames.
