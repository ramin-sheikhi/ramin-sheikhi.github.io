About me

From design offices to multi-hazard research.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Structural Engineering at Ozyegin University, Istanbul, advised by Asst. Prof. Derya Deniz. My thesis examines the multi-hazard performance of structural buildings considering fires and debris flows after earthquakes.

Before my doctorate I spent six years as a structural engineer designing and supervising reinforced concrete and steel buildings, and I carried out field reconnaissance of industrial facilities after the 2023 Kahramanmaraş earthquake sequence.

Research interests

  • 01Multi-hazard performance and risk assessment of structures (fire following earthquake, debris flows)
  • 02Seismic and thermal analysis using computational methods (OpenSees, OpenSeesPy)
  • 03Structural health monitoring and damage assessment
  • 04Ground motion prediction and uncertainty quantification
  • 05Performance-based seismic design of industrial buildings
